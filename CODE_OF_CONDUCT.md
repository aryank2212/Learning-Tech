# Code of Conduct

Learning-Tech is for people who are learning, and everyone starts as a beginner. This page says how we treat each other. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## What we expect
- **Be kind and patient.** Questions that seem basic to you were hard for you once.
- **Be specific and constructive.** Say what's wrong and how it could be better.
- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are honest mistakes.
- **Respect people's differences:** background, identity, experience, opinions and learning pace.
- **Give credit.** Link to sources and say who did the work.

## What we don't accept
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination
- Sexual or violent language and imagery
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam, advertising unrelated to learning, or pirated material
- Encouraging people to attack systems they don't own (see the ethics notes in the networking and security subjects)

## Scope
This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that belongs to the project.

## Reporting and enforcement
If you see behaviour that breaks these rules, open an issue marked as a conduct concern, or contact the maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who repeatedly break the rules. We'll look at every report carefully and keep the reporter's details private where we can.

## Learning from mistakes
We'd rather explain than punish. If you get something wrong, a maintainer will say so plainly, and you'll get a chance to put it right.
