## Papers worth reading (in order)

1. Rumelhart et al., *Learning representations by back-propagating errors* (1986)
2. Krizhevsky et al., *ImageNet Classification with Deep CNNs* (AlexNet, 2012)
3. Vaswani et al., *Attention Is All You Need* (2017)
4. Radford et al., *Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners* (GPT-2, 2019)
5. Brown et al., *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners* (GPT-3, 2020)
6. Hu et al., *LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models* (2021)
7. Lewis et al., *Retrieval-Augmented Generation* (2020)

Papers are at https://arxiv.org or https://paperswithcode.com (now https://huggingface.co/papers).
