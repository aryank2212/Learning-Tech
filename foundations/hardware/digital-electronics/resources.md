# Digital electronics resources

## Books
| | Book | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐🆓 | *The Elements of Computing Systems* (Nand2Tetris) — Nisan & Schocken | [Free course and book](https://www.nand2tetris.org/); builds a computer from NAND gates |
| 💲 | *Digital Design and Computer Architecture* — Harris & Harris | Has RISC-V and MIPS editions |
| 💲 | *Digital Fundamentals* — Thomas Floyd | Clear, very practical |
| 💲 | *Fundamentals of Digital Logic with Verilog Design* — Brown & Vranesic | Good Verilog coverage |
| 💲 | *Digital Design: With an Introduction to the Verilog HDL* — Mano & Ciletti | Classic textbook |
| 💲 | *Code* — Charles Petzold | Friendly story of how computers are built from logic |

## Courses and videos
