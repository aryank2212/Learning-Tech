# Practice: Maths

Work through the levels in order. Spend at least 30 minutes on a task before looking anything up. At the end, a short quiz checks the key ideas, and each answer is hidden until you click it.

How to check yourself: write tests for each task, or compare your output with a known answer. For maths and theory, check by hand and then with code.

## Level 1 — Easy (warm-up)

- [ ] Solve 2x + 3 = 11.
- [ ] Compute the determinant of a 2×2 matrix.
- [ ] Write the truth table for p → q.

## Level 2 — Medium (apply it)

- [ ] Prove by induction that the sum of the first n odd numbers is n².
- [ ] Find the eigenvalues of [[2, 1], [1, 2]].
- [ ] Find the probability of exactly two heads in three fair coin flips.

## Level 3 — Hard (build and explain)

- [ ] Prove that √2 is irrational.
- [ ] Derive the gradient-descent update for least squares, then implement it.

## Quick quiz

Answer each question out loud or on paper first, then open the answer.

1. What is the determinant of [[1, 2], [3, 4]]?

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary>

   1·4 − 2·3 = −2.

   </details>

2. What is the derivative of sin(x²)?

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary>

   2x·cos(x²), by the chain rule.

   </details>

3. How many ways are there to choose 3 items from 5?

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary>

   C(5, 3) = 10.

   </details>

4. State the principle of mathematical induction.

   <details><summary>Show answer</summary>

   If P(0) is true, and P(k) implies P(k+1) for every k, then P(n) is true for every n.

   </details>
