# Theory of computation resources

## Books
| | Book | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐💲 | *Introduction to the Theory of Computation* — Michael Sipser | The clearest modern textbook |
| 💲 | *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation* — Hopcroft, Motwani, Ullman | The classic; very thorough |
| 💲 | *Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness* — Garey & Johnson | The catalogue of NP-complete problems |
| 🆓 | *Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach* — Arora & Barak | Advanced; free PDF from the authors' site (search the title) |
| 💲 | *Elements of the Theory of Computation* — Lewis & Papadimitriou | Concise and rigorous |
| 💲 | *The Nature of Computation* — Moore & Mertens | Broad, readable, with history |
| 💲 | *Quantum Computing Since Democritus* — Scott Aaronson | Lively essays; touches complexity and limits |

## Tools
- [JFLAP](https://www.jflap.org/) — draw and simulate automata, grammars and Turing machines
- [Automata Tutor](https://automatatutor.com/) — interactive automata exercises
- [Turing machine simulator (Anthony Morphett)](https://morphett.info/turing/turing.html) — run machines in the browser
- [Graphviz](https://graphviz.org/) — draw automata from code
- [Lean 4](https://lean-lang.org/) and [Coq](https://coq.inria.fr/) — proof assistants

## Reference and reading
- [Complexity Zoo](https://complexityzoo.net/) — the catalogue of complexity classes
- [Shtetl-Optimized (Scott Aaronson's blog)](https://scottaaronson.blog/) — complexity, quantum and the limits of computation

## Classic papers
- Turing, *On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem* (1936)
- Rabin & Scott, *Finite Automata and Their Decision Problems* (1959)
- Cook, *The Complexity of Theorem-Proving Procedures* (1971)
- Karp, *Reducibility Among Combinatorial Problems* (1972)
- Savitch, *Relationships Between Nondeterministic and Deterministic Tape Complexities* (1970)
