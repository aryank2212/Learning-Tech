# Practice

Practice sets for every subject, in three levels:

- **Level 1 — Easy:** warm-ups that check you've understood the basics.
- **Level 2 — Medium:** tasks that combine ideas from a phase.
- **Level 3 — Hard:** projects that take days and need you to explain your choices.

Each subject folder has its own `practice.md`. Start at Level 1 even if the subject feels familiar.
