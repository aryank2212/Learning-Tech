# Quantum computing resources

## Books
| | Book | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐💲 | *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information* — Nielsen & Chuang | The standard reference; read the first chapters first |
| ⭐💲 | *Quantum Computing for Computer Scientists* — Yanofsky & Mannucci | Written for programmers and computer scientists |
| 💲 | *Programming Quantum Computers* — Johnston, Harrigan, Gimeno-Segovia | Practical, with code |
| 💲 | *Quantum Computing: An Applied Approach* — Jack Hidary | Applied focus, with Qiskit examples |
| 💲 | *Quantum Computer Science* — N. David Mermin | Short and conceptual |
| 💲 | *Quantum Computing Since Democritus* — Scott Aaronson | Essays on what quantum computers can and cannot do |

## Tools and simulators
- [Qiskit](https://qiskit.org/) and [Qiskit Aer](https://qiskit.github.io/qiskit-aer/) — circuits and simulation
- [Cirq](https://quantumai.google/cirq) — circuits for Google's hardware and simulators
- [PennyLane](https://pennylane.ai/) — quantum machine learning
- [Quirk](https://algassert.com/quirk) — a visual, browser-based simulator
- [IBM Quantum Platform](https://quantum.ibm.com/) — free access to real quantum processors, with queues

## Classic papers
- Deutsch & Jozsa, *Rapid Solution of Problems by Quantum Computation* (1992)
- Shor, *Algorithms for Quantum Computation: Discrete Logarithms and Factoring* — [quant-ph/9508027](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9508027)
- Grover, *A Fast Quantum Mechanical Algorithm for Database Search* — [quant-ph/9605043](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605043)
- Bennett & Brassard, *Quantum Cryptography: Public Key Distribution and Coin Tossing* (1984) — BB84
- Peruzzo et al., *A Variational Eigenvalue Solver on a Photonic Quantum Processor* (VQE) — [1304.3061](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.3061)
- Farhi, Goldstone, Gutmann, *A Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm* (QAOA) — [1411.4028](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.4028)
- Preskill, *Quantum Computing in the NISQ Era and Beyond* — [1801.00862](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00862)
- Abhijith et al., *Quantum Algorithm Implementations for Beginners* — [1804.03719](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03719)
